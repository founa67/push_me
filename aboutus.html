📦 Who we are

Push-Me is a hands-on training studio based in Bremen's Überseestadt. We teach Git & GitHub the way we wish someone had taught us: without gatekeeping, with real-world workflows, and a lot of empathy.

Founded in 2021 by two former platform engineers, we've helped over 600 developers, designers, and product folks from northern Germany (and beyond) feel at home in version control. Whether you're a solo maker or part of a 200-person team, we'll get you pushing with confidence.

🛠️ What we do

We offer public workshops, private team trainings, and 1:1 coaching — all in German or English. Our courses cover:

  • Git foundations – commits, branching, merging, rebasing
  • GitHub collaboration – pull requests, code review, actions
  • Advanced rescue – reflog, bisect, conflict resolution
  • Team workflows – trunk-based, GitFlow, monorepos

Every session is built around your repository, not boring slides. You'll break things, fix them, and leave with muscle memory.

🧭 Why Bremen?

Bremen is our home — a city of short distances, strong coffee, and a growing tech scene. We love the Hanseatic pragmatism: no fluff, just work that works. That's exactly how we teach Git: straightforward, reliable, and ready for the real world.

We also run a monthly free "Git Stammtisch" at a local café near the Schlachte — everyone's welcome, from absolute beginners to rebase wizards.

💡 Our values

  • 🔀 No fear — We turn "oh no, I broke it" into "I got this".
  • 🌿 Branch out — Learn by doing, not by memorizing commands.
  • 🤝 Pull together — Collaboration is a skill, and we practice it.
